The Client Gap

Six trust breakers no GPU cloud can solve today without chip-level encryption.

Six reasons your top AI deals walk — to private builds, sovereign vendors, or no deal at all. One root cause: clients don't trust the cloud, and today's GPU clouds can't cryptographically prove they should.

01 · Cross-border data
"My data cannot leave its jurisdiction."
EU records, US ITAR, KSA data, India PII — hard residency rules. Today's only safe answer is a private build at home.
02 · Regulator proof
"Prove no one in the cloud can see inside."
HIPAA, DORA, GDPR, FedRAMP auditors want cryptographic proof — not contracts. Shared GPU infra cannot produce it.
03 · Model & IP
"My model weights are the company."
A frontier model or fine-tune is worth $10M–$1B. Clients won't leave it in plaintext on a host they don't control.
04 · Multi-party AI
"Two parties want to train. Neither will share the data."
Bank + bank, hospital + pharma, gov + vendor. The deal dies without verifiable mutual non-access.
05 · Audit & evidence
"Show me what ran, on what GPU, on what data."
Regulated workloads need a signed trail of every job — model, data, silicon. Standard cloud logs don't cut it.
06 · Vendor concentration
"We will not put sovereign AI in one US hyperscaler."
Governments and tier-1 enterprises are mandated off single-vendor AI — and have nowhere to go.
